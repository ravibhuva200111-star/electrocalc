Enter your input voltage and two resistor values to find the output voltage at the midpoint — the classic voltage divider circuit.

Voltage Divider Calculator
Output Voltage (Vout) — V
Current Through Divider — mA
Power Dissipated — mW
Formula: Vout = Vin × R2 / (R1 + R2). Keep in mind: a voltage divider under load will sag if the load draws significant current relative to the divider current — this calculator assumes no load (open circuit output).
