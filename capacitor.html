Capacitor Tool · 004

Three digits in.
Real capacitance out.

Enter the 3-digit code printed on a ceramic capacitor (like 104 or 223) to get its value in picofarads, nanofarads, and microfarads.

Capacitor Code Reader
104
2 digits + multiplier
100,000 pF
= 100 nF = 0.1 µF
How it works: first two digits are significant figures, third digit is the power-of-10 multiplier in picofarads (pF). Example: 104 = 10 × 10⁴ pF = 100,000 pF = 100 nF = 0.1 µF.
